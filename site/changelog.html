How we version

Free within a major version

Every 1.x update is free to 1.x owners, and every 2.x update is free to 2.x owners. PRISM's 1.0 release is free to everyone who bought the 0.9.0 public beta. Parameter IDs are frozen across a major version, so automation written years ago still lands where you put it.
