ANVIL

Transient shaper and saturating bus compressor.

ANVIL does two jobs in one signal path: a transient stage that separates attack from sustain without a detector to fight, and a bus compressor with program-dependent release and a saturation stage that runs after the gain element, not before it. Zero-latency mode for tracking, oversampled mode for mixdown. Version 2.0.1 is a free update for all 1.x owners.
