VANTA

8-voice virtual-analog polysynth

VANTA is our flagship: eight independent voices, each with two oscillators, a resonant ladder filter, and its own small amount of tuning and envelope error, because a perfectly matched voice card has never existed. Unison stacks all eight. The modulation matrix has twenty-four slots and no hidden routing. It is the instrument the playable demo on this site actually runs.

DRIFT

Tape-modelled delay & pitch-warping echo

DRIFT models the parts of tape delay people actually miss: wow and flutter with correlated low-frequency drift, head-gap loss on repeats, and a record path that saturates before it distorts. Push the transport speed and the repeats bend with it, the way a machine does. Tempo-sync, free-run, and a freeze that holds the loop without re-recording into it.

PRISM

4-band spectral filter & formant morpher

PRISM splits the input into four spectral bands you can move, tilt and resonate independently, then lets you drag the formant structure of one band onto another. It is a public beta: the DSP is stable and the preset format is frozen, the interface is not. Buy it now at $69 and the 1.0 release is a free update. Report anything that misbehaves.

ANVIL

Transient shaper & saturating bus compressor

ANVIL does two jobs in one signal path: a transient stage that separates attack from sustain without a detector to fight, and a bus compressor with program-dependent release and a saturation stage that runs after the gain element, not before it. Zero-latency mode for tracking, oversampled mode for mixdown. Version 2.0.1 is a free update for all 1.x owners.

Inside VANTA

What eight voices actually costs

Architecture, modulation, and the parts we refused to fake.

01

Eight real voices

Eight voices, two oscillators each, allocated round-robin with proper release stealing. A held chord keeps its tails when the ninth note arrives.

02

Two filters, one path

A four-pole ladder with self-oscillation and a state-variable filter with continuous LP–BP–HP morph. Series or parallel, with a single blend control.

03

Per-voice analog error

Each voice carries independent tuning, envelope-timing and filter-cutoff offsets. One knob scales the whole spread from dead-accurate to visibly falling apart.

04

24-slot modulation matrix

Every source reaches every destination at audio rate. Slots are listed, not buried: source, destination, depth, curve, four columns, no submenus.

05

MPE and plain MIDI

Full MPE with per-note pitch, pressure and slide, and a legacy mode that maps the same destinations to mod wheel and aftertouch for ordinary keyboards.

06

Presets you can read

Presets are plain text, tagged and diffable, stored in a folder you choose. Copy one into a chat window and it still loads on the other end.
